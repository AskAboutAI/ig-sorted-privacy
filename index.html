IG sorted

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · Applies to the IG sorted browser extension, version 1.1.0 and later

In short: IG sorted organises your Instagram saved posts on your own computer. It has no servers and no accounts. It never asks for your Instagram password, never sends your saved content anywhere, and never sells or shares any data.

Who we are

IG sorted is an independent browser extension. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Instagram or Meta. "Instagram" is a trademark of its owner and is used here only to describe what the extension works with.

What the extension reads

Only after you press Start, and only on the Saved page of Instagram in your own logged-in browser tab, IG sorted reads what Instagram has already shown on screen for each saved post:

  • the link to the post or reel,
  • the thumbnail image address,
  • the image description text Instagram attaches to the thumbnail, and any caption text shown,
  • labels that say whether an item is a reel, carousel or video,
  • the name of the saved collection (for example "All posts").

It does not read your password, cookies, login tokens, direct messages, followers, other people's data, or any website other than instagram.com. It never likes, saves, unsaves, follows, comments or posts anything. If you turn on Auto-scroll, it scrolls your Saved page at a gentle pace so more posts appear, and you can turn this off at any time.

What is stored, and where

Everything stays in your browser on your device, in storage that belongs to the extension:

  • one record per saved item: its link, visible text, hashtags, thumbnail address, suggested category with the reason, and any changes you make;
  • a short history of when you ran a capture and how many items were found;
  • a few preferences, such as whether Auto-scroll is on.

Images and videos are not downloaded or copied. Thumbnails are shown by loading Instagram's own image address, as Instagram does.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent by the extension. IG sorted has no server, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no outside AI service. Your saved posts are sorted on your computer using simple, visible rules. The extension's pages are technically blocked from contacting any other website.

If you choose Download my organised library, an Excel file is created on your computer and saved where you choose. If you share that file, you are sharing the links and captions in it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than organising your saves for you. We never see your data at all.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

The use of information received from Instagram pages adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose: organising your own saved Instagram posts into a private library on your device.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your library stays until you delete it. You can delete it at any time with Reset library at the bottom of the IG sorted page, or by removing the extension, which deletes all of its storage. Nothing changes on your Instagram account either way.

Children

IG sorted is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle data about them. It only works with an Instagram account that is already logged in.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Changes will never allow your data to leave your device without your clear consent.
